Data Deletion Instructions

If you want us to delete the information we hold about you, follow these steps:

  1. Send a message to our Facebook Page, facebook.com/1164771953375202, with the words "Delete my data", from the same account you used to contact us.
  2. We will confirm your identity from the conversation and reply to confirm.
  3. We will delete your messages, order details and contact details from our records within 30 days, except information we must keep by law (for example, accounting records).

You can also remove our access to your Facebook data at any time in your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.
