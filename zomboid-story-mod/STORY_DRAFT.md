# DEAD AIR — A Story Mod Draft for Project Zomboid

> *"If you can hear this, you're not alone. If you can hear this, keep moving."*
> — Looped broadcast, 91.7 FM, first heard July 9, 1993

---

## 0. At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Working title** | *Dead Air* (alt: *Last Frequency*, *The Ashgrove Signal*) |
| **Genre** | Survival mystery / environmental storytelling |
| **Length** | ~30–60 in-game days for the main thread; side stories optional |
| **Tone** | Quiet, lonely, grounded. Hope that costs something. No superheroes, no cure. |
| **Setting** | Knox County Exclusion Zone, Kentucky, July–September 1993 |
| **Delivery** | Radio broadcasts, cassette tapes, handwritten notes, annotated maps, staged scenes (bodies, barricades, campsites), VHS tapes |
| **Protagonist** | The player's own character. The story never assumes their name, job, or gender. |
| **NPCs** | None alive and talking (fits vanilla). Every character is met through what they left behind. |

**Logline:** A weak FM signal promises a way out of the Exclusion Zone. Following it leads the player across Knox County, through the lives of the people who heard it before them — and to the operator who has to decide whether the signal should keep broadcasting at all.

---

## 1. Core Premise

On the first morning, any radio the player turns on can pick up a faint, unofficial station on **91.7 FM**. A tired man's voice calls himself **"Dutch"** and says he is a volunteer ham operator at the **Ashgrove Fire Lookout**, a tower in the woods. He reads out names of survivors who have checked in, gives safe routes, warns of "big crowds" moving along roads, and repeats one promise:

> *"There's a crossing. River's wide but it's not watched everywhere. We're putting together a group. Get to Ashgrove and we'll get you across."*

The story is about following that promise. Along the way the player finds:

1. The **people who followed the signal before them** — their caches, their journals, and, mostly, their bodies.
2. The truth about the **crossing**: it was real once. Then the military started sinking boats.
3. The truth about **Dutch**: he died on Day 6. The broadcast the player hears is his niece, **June Brenner**, playing his tapes and recording new ones in his voice-style, because people stopped coming when she spoke as herself.
4. A final choice: **keep the signal alive, change what it says, or shut it off.**

**Central theme:** *Is a hopeful lie kinder than an honest silence?* Every act should push on that question from a different side.

---

## 2. Lore Fit & Timeline

The mod stays inside established Project Zomboid lore and avoids "explaining" the Knox Infection. The cause is never revealed. Notes may speculate, but nothing confirms it.

| In-game date | Event | What the player experiences |
|---|---|---|
| **Jul 9 (Day 1)** | Game start. Knox Event underway; Exclusion Zone in force. | 91.7 FM broadcasts begin, Dutch's real voice. |
| **Jul 10–14** | Dutch coordinates ~40 survivors by radio. First group crosses the Ohio River near Brandenburg at night. It works. | Broadcasts are confident, specific, full of names. |
| **Jul 15 (Day 6)** | Dutch is bitten while pulling a family up the lookout stairs. Dies that night. June takes over. | Broadcast that evening is short; Dutch sounds sick. Next day the station is silent. |
| **Jul 17** | June starts replaying Dutch's old tapes, then splicing new ones. | Broadcasts return, but routes and names start repeating. Attentive players notice. |
| **Jul 18–22** | Second crossing attempt. Military patrol boats open fire. Most of the group dies on the water. | No broadcast mentions this. |
| **~Jul 25** | Military helicopter event (vanilla). | Optional tie-in: June's notes reference it. |
| **Power/water shutoff** (sandbox-dependent) | June switches the station to a generator and car batteries. | Broadcasts get shorter and crackle more. Fuel becomes a quest beat. |
| **Day 30–60** | Player reaches Ashgrove; endgame. | Finale. |

> **Design note:** Tie broadcasts to *in-game day*, not player progress, so the radio "lives" even while the player ignores it. Story items in the world unlock and change based on the player's progress flags.

## 3. Characters

All characters are met indirectly. Each has a clear voice so players recognize them from a single note.

### Walter "Dutch" Brenner — *The Voice*
- 61, retired lineman for the county power co-op, ham radio hobbyist (callsign written on his notes: **KD4-WXB**; fictional).
- Volunteered at the Ashgrove Fire Lookout every summer. Had his rig set up there when the Event started.
- **Voice:** Slow, warm, practical. Uses "friend" a lot. Never swears on air, swears constantly in his private log.
- **Arc:** Real hero for six days. Saved people. Made one bad call (a crowded stairwell) that killed him.
- **Found via:** Early broadcasts, his logbook at the lookout, a farewell cassette.

### June Brenner — *The Operator*
- 24, Dutch's niece, nursing school dropout, working a summer job at the Muldraugh Spiffo's when the Event hit. Made it to Ashgrove on Day 3.
- **Voice:** Clipped, sharp, sarcastic, exhausted. Writes lists. Crosses things out.
- **Arc:** Kept the station going out of grief and duty, then out of a belief that "a reason to move keeps people alive longer than the truth would." She knows the crossing is deadly now. She has not decided what that makes her.
- **Fate:** Determined by the player (see Endings). She is alive when the player arrives — the only living "character" in the mod, encountered as a locked tower door, a voice through it, and notes passed under it (see §6).

### Ray Okafor — *The First Through*
- 35, long-haul trucker stuck in West Point when the roads closed. Led the first group across the river on Jul 13.
- **Found via:** Nothing at first, because he made it. Mid-game, the player finds a note he left at the Brandenburg boat ramp for the next group: *"Made it. Tell Dutch. Lights on the other side, Indiana side. Don't wait for full dark, they sweep with spotlights after 1."*
- **Purpose:** Proof the crossing *was* real. Keeps June from being simply a liar.

### The Hollis Family — *The Ones Dutch Saved*
- Tom, Carla, and their daughter Bea (9). The family Dutch pulled up the lookout stairs.
- **Found via:** A child's drawing taped inside the lookout ("MR DUTCH IS A HERO"), Carla's diary in a Riverside house.
- **Purpose:** Emotional anchor. Shows the cost of Dutch's death had meaning. Bea's later fate is a side story (§5).

### Sergeant Dale Mercer — *The Other Side of the River*
- Kentucky National Guard, assigned to a river patrol unit. Writes letters he never sends.
- **Found via:** A crashed/abandoned military vehicle near the river; a soaked notebook on a corpse in uniform.
- **Voice:** Formal, then cracking. *"We were told they were infected. Some of them waved."*
- **Purpose:** Gives the military a human face without excusing it. Reveals why the second crossing failed.

### "The Listener" — *The Rival Interpretation*
- Never named. A survivor who decided the broadcast is a trap — a lure to gather survivors in one spot where the dead follow the noise.
- **Found via:** Spray-painted messages across the map ("91.7 = BAIT", "DON'T GO TO THE TOWER"), and a camp full of smashed radios.
- **Purpose:** Introduces doubt early. Players who suspect June's broadcast "feel smart" — and are partly right.

## 4. Main Story — Act by Act

Each chapter lists: **Trigger → Location(s) → What the player finds → What it unlocks.** Locations use vanilla map towns; exact building coordinates are to be picked during level placement.

---

### PROLOGUE — "Static" (Day 1–3)

**Trigger:** Game start. Any radio tuned to 91.7.

**Broadcast 1 (Day 1, every 3 hrs, loops):**
> *"This is KD4-WXB, Ashgrove Lookout, calling anybody in Knox County. My name's Dutch. The TV's going to tell you to stay inside. That's fine advice for a day or two. After that, it's not. If you're hearing this, write down this frequency. I'll read names every evening at seven. If you want your name read, find a way to tell me. Stay quiet. Stay off the main roads. Keep moving. Ashgrove out."*

**Starter world items (spawn-weighted near player start in any town):**
- A **flyer** stapled to a telephone pole / on a fridge: hand-drawn, photocopied. *"91.7 FM — HELP IS ON AIR — Ashgrove Lookout — 7PM NIGHTLY."*
- An **annotated map** (stash map) marking a *"Ashgrove Relay Box"* — the first breadcrumb cache.

**Goal:** Introduce the voice, the frequency, and the idea that someone out there is organizing.

---

### ACT I — "Names Read at Seven" (Days 2–10)

**Chapter 1: The Relay Box**
- **Location:** A rural mailbox / utility box on a back road between Muldraugh and West Point.
- **Finds:** A coffee can with supplies (canned food, bandages, batteries) and a **sign-in sheet**. Twelve names, dates, and "heading to Ashgrove." Some later names are written in shaky hands. The last entry is a child's: *"Bea H. — age 9 — we are going to the tower!!"*
- **Unlocks:** Map annotation → **Rosewood Fire Station** (Dutch's broadcasts mention "the boys at Rosewood left gear for anyone who needs it").

**Chapter 2: Rosewood Fire Station**
- **Finds:** A gear cache (axe, fire jacket, flashlight) and a **cassette tape** labeled *"For the station — D."*. It's Dutch recording a message for firefighters who never came back. He talks about the crossing for the first time in detail: *"Old Mill Road down to the river. Johnboats at the Brandenburg ramp. Go at night."*
- **Environmental scene:** Fire truck half out of the bay, three zombified firefighters in gear nearby (higher toughness, slower).
- **Unlocks:** Knowledge flag `KNOWS_CROSSING`. Evening broadcasts now mention "the river group."

**Chapter 3: The First Doubt**
- **Trigger:** Player has `KNOWS_CROSSING` and in-game day ≥ 7.
- **Finds:** Spray paint on a billboard or water tower: **"91.7 = BAIT. THEY WANT YOU ALL IN ONE PLACE."** A nearby campsite: tent, cold firepit, six smashed radios, a note:

> *"Listened every night for a week. Same names. SAME NAMES. Tues he read Garrett Pike. Thurs he read Garrett Pike. Garrett Pike is in my truck bed with a hole in his head since Monday. Nobody is reading anything. It's a tape. Don't go."*

- **Purpose:** The player now *listens differently*. If they compare broadcasts, they will find repeats from Day 6 onward. (This is deliberate — write repeated lines into the radio script.)

**Chapter 4: The Hollis House (Riverside)**
- **Finds:** Carla Hollis's diary. Last entries:

> *July 14. We're going. Tom says it's two days on foot if we stay in the woods. Bea packed her Spiffo and nothing else. I made her take socks.*
>
> *July 15 — written later, different pen — We made it. Dutch got us up the stairs. He's hurt. He says it's nothing. June won't look at me.*

- **Unlocks:** Map annotation → **Ashgrove Lookout** location (deep woods, reachable from a dirt road). The player now knows where the tower is.

**Act I ending beat:** On Day 10 (or when the player reaches the Hollis house, whichever is later), the 7 PM broadcast changes. Dutch's voice reads names — including **Tom, Carla, and Bea Hollis** — as "safe at Ashgrove." Players who read the diary know Dutch was dying. The voice sounds exactly the same. Something is wrong.

---

### ACT II — "The Crossing" (Days 10–25)

The player can now head straight to the lookout or go to the river first. Both orders work; the reveals are written to stand on their own.

**Chapter 5: The Brandenburg Ramp**
- **Location:** River edge near Brandenburg (the Ohio River boundary).
- **Finds (two layers):**
  1. **Ray Okafor's note**, nailed to the ramp sign, weathered but legible (see §3). The crossing worked on Jul 13.
  2. Below it, down the bank: **three swamped johnboats**, bullet holes in the hulls, bodies tangled in the reeds (some reanimated, standing in shallow water). A child's Spiffo plush caught in a snag.
- **Mood note:** Do not add music. Just water and flies.

**Chapter 6: Mercer's Notebook**
- **Location:** Abandoned military checkpoint or patrol vehicle within a short walk of the ramp.
- **Finds:** Sgt. Mercer's waterlogged notebook on a uniformed corpse (zombie or dead). Key entries:

> *Jul 13. Overnight a boat got through. We found it empty on the Indiana bank at dawn. CO lost his mind. New orders: any vessel leaving the Kentucky shore is to be treated as an infection risk. Warning shots first.*
>
> *Jul 19. There were no warning shots.*
>
> *Jul 19, later. A man in the second boat was holding up a radio. He was yelling a number. Ninety-one point something. I don't know why that's the thing I can't stop thinking about.*
>
> *Jul 22. They're pulling us back from the river. Someone said the fence is moving north. I'm not going.*

- **Unlocks:** Flag `KNOWS_MASSACRE`. The player now knows the crossing is a death trap, and the broadcast is still sending people to it.

**Chapter 7: The Listener's Last Camp** *(optional but recommended)*
- **Location:** A hunting cabin or barn east of the river.
- **Finds:** The Listener's final journal and their body (suicide or bitten — writer's choice; recommend bitten, self-isolated, door barricaded from inside). Final entry:

> *I was half right. It's not a trap. It's worse. It's somebody who believes it.*

- **Purpose:** The rival theory resolves into sympathy. Now the player is the only person alive who knows the whole picture.

**Chapter 8: Generator Problems** *(triggers after power shutoff)*
- **Broadcast change:** Static-heavy. A new line appears, clearly not Dutch — a woman's voice, quickly cut off:
> *"—is this on? Okay. Okay, okay, okay. Uh—"* *[tape splice, Dutch's voice resumes mid-sentence]*
- **World change:** Fuel cans start disappearing from gas stations near Ashgrove; notes on pump handles in June's handwriting: *"Took 2. Sorry. Needed for the radio. — J."*
- **Purpose:** First direct evidence of June. Builds towards meeting her.

---

### ACT III — "Ashgrove" (Day 20+, player-driven)

**Chapter 9: The Approach**
- **Location:** Ashgrove Lookout — a custom-built fire tower in dense forest (tall wooden/steel tower, ground cabin, generator shed, antenna mast). Recommend placing it in the forested area between Riverside and Rosewood, or as a custom map cell.
- **Environmental storytelling at the base:**
  - A **mass of zombies** (sandbox-configurable: 20–80) drawn by months of radio noise, milling around the tower. The broadcast is literally attracting the dead. (This is the Listener's theory, partially true.)
  - A ring of **graves** with hand-painted markers. One says *"DUTCH — KD4-WXB — SIGNING OFF."*
  - Hollis family belongings at the ground cabin. **No Hollis bodies.** (See side story.)
  - The **generator shed**, out of fuel or nearly so.

**Chapter 10: Dutch's Logbook**
- **Location:** Ground cabin.
- **Finds:** Dutch's private radio log. Mix of technical entries and confession.

> *Jul 11. 14 checked in. Pike boys, Okafor, the Delgado sisters. Weather clear. Antenna holding.*
>
> *Jul 13, 0340. Okafor's light flashed twice from the far bank. Two flashes means across. I sat down on the floor and cried like a baby. June saw. She can tell people if she wants.*
>
> *Jul 15. Got nipped on the stairs. Hollis girl's fine. That's the trade, and I'd make it again. Recorded a few tapes for June to play so people don't panic if I'm off the air a day or two.*
>
> *Jul 15, late. She knows. I told her: if the river closes, quit sending folks. Promise me. She said okay. She said it too fast.*

- **Finds (also):** Box of labeled cassettes — *"Evening names (generic)"*, *"Routes – north"*, *"Routes – river"*, *"Weather – fill"*. The player now understands how the broadcast is made.

**Chapter 11: June**
- **Location:** The lookout cab at the top of the tower. The stairs are barricaded halfway up with a hatch.
- **Interaction design (no NPC AI needed):**
  - The player knocks / interacts with the hatch → a **context menu** opens: "Call out", "Slide a note under the hatch", "Leave".
  - June responds through **text popups / notes pushed back down** (pre-written; branch on the player's flags).
  - If the player has `KNOWS_MASSACRE`, they can slide **Mercer's notebook** through. This unlocks the full ending choice. Without it, June refuses to open up and says the crossing "still works, you just have to be careful," and the player must go find proof.

**June's key note (after reading Mercer's notebook):**
> *I knew. Not about the boats. About the patrols. I heard gunfire on Jul 19 from up here. You can hear the river at night.*
>
> *I kept playing the tapes because every night I play them, somebody walks instead of sitting in a closet waiting to die. Some of them get here. Some of them stay. Five people are buried down there who'd have died anyway, and three are alive in Indiana because of him, and I don't know how to do the math on the rest.*
>
> *He made me promise. I said okay too fast.*
>
> *You read the notebook. You tell me what it says next.*

---

### FINALE — "Sign-Off" (Player Choice)

The player interacts with the **transmitter** in the tower cab. Three options (context menu), each with its own final broadcast that plays on 91.7 for the rest of the save, and its own world consequences.

#### Ending A — "Keep Moving" *(The Honest Signal)*
- Player and June record a new message together. June's own voice, finally.
> *"This is June Brenner at Ashgrove. My uncle Dutch ran this station. He died on July fifteenth. I've been playing his tapes since. I'm sorry. The river crossing is closed — the army is shooting boats. Do not go to the river. Ashgrove has water, a fence, and room. If you come, come quiet. If you can't come, stay alive where you are. I'll read names at seven. Real ones. Ashgrove out."*
- **Consequences:**
  - Tower becomes a **safehouse** (sandbox: spawns occasional supply drops / "survivor gifts" at the base — notes from other listeners, food, as the story's "community" grows).
  - Zombie horde at base slowly increases (the noise never stops). Tension between hope and danger stays.
  - Achievement-style text: *"The names are real now."*

#### Ending B — "The Promise" *(Silence)*
- Player shuts the transmitter off. June doesn't stop them.
> Final broadcast (played once, then nothing): *Dutch's tape: "...if you're hearing this, keep moving. Ashgrove out." — then dead air.*
- **Consequences:**
  - Radio is silent forever on 91.7.
  - The horde around the tower slowly disperses over ~2 weeks.
  - June leaves a final note in the cabin and is gone: *"Going to look for the Hollis family. Kept one tape. Not the routes one. The one where he laughs."*
  - Bittersweet, quiet. Safest outcome for the player.

#### Ending C — "Dead Air" *(Keep the Lie)*
- Player chooses to restart the old tapes, river route and all. (Only available if the player gives June enough fuel — the choice is active, not passive.)
- **Consequences:**
  - Broadcasts continue, unchanged, forever.
  - June stays in the tower. Over the following weeks, new **survivor stashes** appear around the map (people still coming), and new **bodies** appear at the Brandenburg ramp.
  - A final note from June, weeks later, tucked under the hatch: *"Four came today. One asked about the river. I told her to wait for the weather. I've been saying that a lot."*
  - Darkest ending. No judgment in the text. The player decides how to feel.

> **Optional secret ending — "Two Flashes":** If the player finds all three side-story items (§5) *and* chooses Ending A, a final world event spawns weeks later at the Brandenburg ramp: a lantern on the Indiana bank, flashing twice. No explanation.

## 5. Side Stories

Each is self-contained, 1–3 locations, and rewards exploration. Each ends in an item that matters to the finale.

### 5.1 "Bea's Spiffo" — *The Hollis Family*
- The Hollises left Ashgrove on Jul 17 for the second crossing. They never reached the river — Tom turned back halfway because Bea was sick.
- Trail: Spiffo's restaurant in Muldraugh (June's old workplace — June's apron, a note to a coworker) → a church in March Ridge with a chalk message on the door: *"HOLLIS — 3 — GOING NORTH. BEA OK."* → an empty, tidy house near Louisville's edge with a fresh drawing: the tower, a radio, and three stick figures waving.
- **Outcome:** They're probably alive, somewhere. Never confirmed.
- **Reward:** *Bea's drawing* (story item). Giving it to June unlocks an extra line in Endings A and B.

### 5.2 "Callsign" — *The Ham Radio Network*
- Dutch wasn't the only ham operator. Three other operators (Louisville, Ekron, a farmhouse near Echo Creek) talked with him nightly.
- Each site has a dead rig and a logbook. Together they reveal that the outside world knows about Ashgrove — and that someone in Indiana was relaying messages back for the first week.
- **Reward:** *Relay Codebook* — the "two flashes" signal code. Needed for the secret ending.

### 5.3 "Okafor's Load" — *The Trucker's Rig*
- Ray Okafor's semi is jackknifed on a highway outside West Point. His cab has a CB radio log, family photos, and a half-written letter to his daughter.
- Trailer contains a large cache (canned goods / water) — a big survival reward in mid-game.
- **Reward:** *Ray's letter*. Lets June read his name on the honest broadcast in Ending A ("Ray Okafor — made it across — July thirteenth").

### 5.4 "Pike Brothers" *(small, dark)*
- Garrett Pike (from the Listener's note) and his brother Danny. Danny kept going to Ashgrove after Garrett died. Danny's name is on a grave at the tower base. The two brothers' stories are in two places on the map; finding both reveals Danny made it to Ashgrove, then volunteered for the second crossing.

## 6. Implementation Notes (for the mod build)

This section is a planning guide, not exact code. Check every API name against the Project Zomboid version you target (Build 41 vs Build 42 changed some systems).

### 6.1 Suggested mod layout
```
DeadAir/
├─ mod.info
├─ poster.png
├─ media/
│  ├─ scripts/
│  │   └─ DeadAir_items.txt          -- story items (notes, tapes, logbooks, drawing)
│  ├─ lua/
│  │   ├─ shared/
│  │   │   └─ Translate/EN/          -- all story text lives here (easy to translate)
│  │   ├─ client/
│  │   │   ├─ DeadAir_ReadUI.lua     -- popup window to read notes/logs
│  │   │   └─ DeadAir_Hatch.lua      -- context menu for June's hatch & transmitter
│  │   └─ server/
│  │       ├─ DeadAir_State.lua      -- story flags, saved in ModData
│  │       ├─ DeadAir_Stashes.lua    -- annotated maps / caches
│  │       └─ DeadAir_Events.lua     -- day-based world changes
│  ├─ radio/                         -- custom 91.7 FM broadcast scripts
│  ├─ sound/                         -- (optional) voiced tapes
│  └─ maps/                          -- (optional) Ashgrove Lookout custom cell
```

### 6.2 Systems to use
| Need | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Story state** | Store flags (`KNOWS_CROSSING`, `KNOWS_MASSACRE`, `MET_JUNE`, `ENDING`) in global ModData so they persist in saves and sync in multiplayer. |
| **Breadcrumb caches** | Vanilla annotated-map / stash system (stash descriptions) — a map item marks a location and spawns a container with fixed contents. Perfect fit for Relay Box, Rosewood, etc. |
| **Notes & logbooks** | Custom literature items with an `OnRead`-style hook that opens a scrolling text window. Keep all text in the Translate files. |
| **Cassettes / VHS** | Either reuse the vanilla recorded-media system (if targeting a build that supports custom tapes) or use items that play a sound + show subtitles when used with a cassette player/radio. |
| **91.7 FM broadcast** | Custom radio channel with day-scheduled broadcast lines. Write repeating lines on purpose from Day 6 (the "tape" clue). Change content based on the ending flag. |
| **Day-based changes** | `Events.EveryDays` / `Events.EveryHours` to check the in-game day and flip world state (fuel notes, new bodies, supply drops). |
| **Staged scenes** | Spawn corpses/zombies with specific outfits at fixed coordinates when the player first loads that chunk (check a "spawned" flag so it only happens once). |
| **June's hatch** | `OnFillWorldObjectContextMenu` → add options when the player right-clicks the hatch tile. Show branching text with a simple dialog panel. |
| **Sandbox options** | Horde size at tower, how often broadcasts play, whether endings change the world, "story items only spawn once" toggle. |

### 6.3 Multiplayer considerations
- Story flags should be **server-wide**: one ending per server.
- Notes should spawn once per world, not per player. Optionally let every player read the same physical copy.
- The finale should require only one player at the transmitter, but announce the choice to everyone (a final broadcast is a natural way to do that).

### 6.4 Build order (smallest playable version first)
1. **Vertical slice:** Radio Day 1 broadcast + Relay Box stash + one note. Prove the loop works.
2. Add Act I caches and the "repeated names" radio script.
3. Build Ashgrove Lookout (can start as an existing vanilla building re-dressed before making a custom tower).
4. Add Mercer's notebook + the hatch dialog + three endings.
5. Side stories, voice acting, custom map cell, polish.

**Grave marker at Ashgrove:**
> *DANNY PIKE — CAME ALL THE WAY — WENT ALL THE WAY.*

**Dutch's farewell cassette (found in Ending B, or in the logbook box):**
> *"June-bug. If you're playing this, I'm not around to argue with you, so you get to win one. Here's the thing I didn't say on air. Hope's a tool. Like a pry bar. You use it to open a door, then you put it down. You don't carry it around swinging it at people. Okay. That's all. Go eat something. KD4-WXB, signing off."*

## 8. Open Questions for You (the modder)

1. **Build target:** Build 41 or Build 42? This changes how radio, tapes, and items should be built.
2. **Custom map or vanilla only?** A custom Ashgrove tower cell is the biggest single task. A vanilla rural house re-dressed with props works for an early version.
3. **Voice acting?** Dutch's tapes are strong candidates for real audio; everything else works fine as text.
4. **Should June be killable?** Recommendation: no — she is behind a hatch for a reason. But a "June turned" variant for players who arrive very late (Day 90+) could be a powerful extra ending.
5. **Name:** *Dead Air* is the working title. If it's taken on the Workshop, *Ashgrove Signal* is the backup.
