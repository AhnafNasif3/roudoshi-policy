Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Roudoshi ("we", "us") is a clothing business in Bangladesh that sells through its Facebook Page, facebook.com/1164771953375202. This policy explains what information we handle through our Facebook app ("Claude API"), which we use only for our own Page and ad account.

Information we handle

  • Messages that customers send to our Page on Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp, including any name, phone number, delivery address and order details a customer chooses to share.
  • Public information about our Page, such as posts, comments, reactions and aggregated insights.
  • Aggregated advertising results for our own ad account, such as reach, clicks and spend.

How we use it

  • To answer customer questions and process and deliver orders.
  • To understand which posts and ads work, and to run our own advertising.
  • To help draft replies. We may use automated tools, including AI services, to suggest replies. Customer information is used only for this purpose, never to train models for other businesses.

Sharing

We do not sell customer information. We share delivery details (name, phone, address) with courier services only to deliver an order, and we share information with Meta and our service providers only as needed to operate our Page, ads and messaging, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed to handle orders, returns and customer support, or as required by law.

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you. See our data deletion instructions.
